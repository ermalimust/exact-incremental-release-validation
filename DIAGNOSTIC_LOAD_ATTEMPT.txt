Excluded load-feasibility attempt
An initial single-host attempt used 120 seconds of measurement, nominal 120
updates/s, H=4096, support size 512, fanout 32, 20 arrivals/s and four workers.
Its first Full SQL run failed because the serial source injector did not finish
within the 60-second drain allowance. All four workers exited normally; the
saved source log contains 11273 acknowledged events. The last acknowledged event
had a planned offset about 95.9 s and actual occurrence about 184 s after run start.
This is a driver/load-feasibility diagnostic, not a completed policy comparison
or a service-capacity estimate. No observation from it enters the formal CIs.
The full failed run is retained in the authors' local diagnostic archive; its
large raw files and internal review notes are not included in this public ZIP.
The final design, specified before completed comparative long-service cells
were inspected, uses 120 s, nominal 40 updates/s and H=1024 with the other settings
unchanged. All three policies receive that same final external workload.
